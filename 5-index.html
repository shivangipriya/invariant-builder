Invariant Builder

Describe a feature. Get candidate invariants - things that might need to stay true when it runs - each with its assumptions shown. Nothing here is a requirement until you accept it. Free, nothing uploaded: everything runs in this page.

Candidate invariants

Every card starts at Not sure. Accept only what you have checked against how your product actually works. The extraction is pattern-based: it will miss things and suggest things that do not fit.

Prompts for what may be missing

These are questions to ask yourself, not proof that anything is unprotected.

Possible wording conflicts

Pairs of your sentences that may clash - or may differ by actor, scope, or time (for example admins vs everyone, drafts vs published). Only you can tell which.

Your accepted spec

Markdown of the candidates you accepted, with their assumptions. Paste it into a ticket, your repo, or your AI coding agent's instructions.

What this tool does not do

  • It does not verify any code. It helps you write down what correct means; you or your tests still have to check it.
  • It does not know your business rules. Every candidate carries a visible assumption; if the assumption is wrong for your product, the candidate is wrong. That is why each one starts unconfirmed and shows an alternative policy.
  • Extraction is pattern-based and will sometimes be wrong. Cards triggered by your sentences are suggestions, not requirements read out of your text.
  • Other tools work nearby: property-based testing libraries, spec languages, AI coding assistants. This is the plain-language step before those.
  • The tool sends nothing you type anywhere. What you paste is processed on your device and disappears when you leave.
